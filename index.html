🎸 How to Play Pocket Guitar

📱 Touch Controls

  • Upper Area (Frets / Chords): Press frets or tap chord pads with your fretting hand.
  • Lower Area (Strum Zone): Swipe horizontally across the strings to strum, or tap individual strings to pick arpeggios.

🔀 Two Play Modes

  • Fretboard Mode: Press directly on the 6 strings (6E on the left to 1E on the right, or reversed in Lefty mode) across frets 1–5. Use Guide to highlight chord shapes, and toggle Hold: ON to latch frets without holding your fingers down.
  • Chords Mode: Tap any chord pad (C, G, Am, F, etc.) to automatically fret that voicing so you can focus on strumming rhythm below.

💻 Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Number keys 1–9: Select chords (C, G, Am, F, Em, D, Dm, E7, A)
  • Space or ↓: Down Strum / ↑: Up Strum
  • Q W E R T Y: Pluck individual strings from left to right
