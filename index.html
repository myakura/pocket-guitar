🎸 Quick Guide

📱 Playing on Touch

  • Upper Deck: Fret notes or select a chord pad with one hand.
  • Lower Deck: Swipe across the strings to strum, or tap individual strings to pluck arpeggios.

🔀 Modes & Ergonomics

  • Fretboard: Multi-touch frets 1–5 across all 6 strings (6E–1E). Turn on Latch to keep notes held after lifting your fingers, or pick a Guide chord to light up its fingering.
  • Chords: One-tap chord pads so you can focus purely on rhythm and strumming.
  • Neck & Lefty: Adjust Neck width (50%–100%) to match real string spacing on large phones, or flip string order with Lefty.

⌨️ Desktop Shortcuts

  • 1–9: Select chords (C, G, Am, F, Em, D, Dm, E7, A)
  • Space / ↓: Downstroke · ↑: Upstroke
  • Q W E R T Y: Pluck strings from left to right
